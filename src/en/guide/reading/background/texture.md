# Background Texture

Background textures are a special type of image — carefully crafted textured graphics that transform flat pixels into breathable paper-like surfaces, designed to soften the stark cold feel of digital screens.

You may fine-tune background textures via the `Opacity` setting for optimal visuals. They can also be combined with [Background Color](/guide/reading/background/color).
